# Letter templates

The writing step fills these templates from the enclosure list only, never from the evidence index. The enclosure list holds the included records only: a description, the record type and how each is provided. The evidence index is internal and is never sent, because it quotes passages from excluded and undecided files that can be about other people. Square-bracket fields are filled from the request input and the enclosure list; the wording around them stays as written. Nothing is added that is not in the enclosure list, and no other person is ever named.

## Template A. HIPAA access response

Date: [date of letter]

To: [person's full name]

Re: Your request for access to your health information, received [date received]

We have completed a search of our records for information about you. The search covered our document, scanned-form, photo and recording folders and was carried out on our own system. [If the on-device model setting was confirmed at setup:] Your records were not sent to any outside service.

We located [number of included files] records. They are listed in the enclosed list. [number of copies] are enclosed as copies. [If extracts exist:] [number of extracts] records also contain information about other patients; for those we enclose an extract with only the entries about you. [If any record is withheld:] The following records are not enclosed: [list]. The reason is [basis for the denial]. [If the denial is reviewable:] You may ask for this decision to be reviewed by a licensed health care professional who was not involved in it, by writing to [contact]. You may also complain to us at [contact] or to the Secretary of the US Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights.

[How the records are provided, for example on an encrypted USB drive, by secure email, or through a protected download link that expires on [date].]

[If unreadable files exist:] We also located [number] files we could not open automatically and have reviewed them by hand: [outcome].

If you have questions about this response, contact [practice contact].

[Signature block]

## Template B. GDPR Article 15 access response

Date: [date of letter]

To: [person's full name]

Re: Your subject access request received [date received]

In response to your request under Article 15 of the General Data Protection Regulation, we confirm that we hold personal data about you. A copy is enclosed, with a list of the [number of included files] records it covers. Where a record also contains other people's data, we enclose an extract of the parts about you, as Article 15(4) requires. The search covered our document, scanned-form, photo and recording folders and was performed on our own system. [If the on-device model setting was confirmed at setup:] No data was sent to an outside service for this search. [How the copy is provided, for example on an encrypted USB drive, by secure email, or through a protected download link that expires on [date].]

[If camera recordings of you were confirmed:] Our cameras recorded you on [dates]. [How the footage is provided, with other people obscured, or why it is not.]

Purposes of processing: [purposes]. Recipients: [recipients or "none outside the organisation"]. Retention period: [period or criteria]. Source of the data: [source]. Automated decision-making, including profiling: [none, or details]. [If data was transferred outside the EU or UK:] Safeguards for that transfer: [safeguards]. You have the right to rectification, erasure, restriction and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

[If withheld or redacted items exist:] The following items are withheld or redacted because [reason]: [list].

[Signature block]

## Template C. GDPR Article 17 erasure response

Date: [date of letter]

To: [person's full name]

Re: Your erasure request received [date received]

In response to your request under Article 17 of the General Data Protection Regulation, we searched our records and identified [number of included files] items containing your personal data.

The following have been deleted from our working files: [list of file descriptions]. Deleted items stay in our system's recycle bin until [date the recycle window ends], and copies in our backups are overwritten by [date from the backup schedule]; they are not used in the meantime.

[If records with other people's data exist:] The following records also concern other people. Your entries have been removed from them by hand: [list].

[If retained items exist:] The following items are retained because we are legally required to keep them ([legal ground under Article 17(3), for example the retention period for health or accounting records]): [list]. They will be deleted when that period ends.

[If a surveillance identity was found:] A face identity in our camera system was removed by our staff on [date]. [If camera recordings of you were found:] Recordings of you are deleted by [date], when the camera retention period ends.

The search was performed on our own system. [If the on-device model setting was confirmed at setup:] No data was transferred to an outside service for this purpose. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

[Signature block]

## Rules for filling

- "File descriptions" are the descriptions from the enclosure list (for example "intake form dated 14 January 2025"), not file paths. Each description was checked in step 5 to name no person.
- The letter never names other people found in the same files.
- The hand-edited, delivery and camera sentences are filled by the user after doing the manual step; until then the brackets stay.
- If the writing delegation does not complete, the unfilled template is saved next to the evidence index and the user fills it.
